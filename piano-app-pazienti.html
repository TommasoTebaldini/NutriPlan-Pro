📱 Freemium Plan — Patient App

Competitive analysis and structure of the paid model. Updated: May 2025

Guiding principle: It makes no sense to charge for features already available for free on Yazio, FatSecret, Cronometer or MyFitnessPal. The value of the Pro plan must be exclusive and hard to replicate — i.e. tied to the dietitian/patient system that only NutriPlan can offer.

📋 Proposed Free vs Pro structure

🔓 Free Plan

Available to everyone, always. Must be useful enough to drive daily use of the app.

  • ✅ Food diary (7-day history)
  • ✅ Calories and macronutrients (protein, fat, carbs, fiber)
  • ✅ CREA+BDA food database (Italian)
  • ✅ Food barcode scanning
  • ✅ Body weight logging
  • ✅ View of the dietitian's meal plan
  • ✅ Chat with your dietitian
  • ✅ Access to questionnaires sent by the dietitian
  • ✅ Meal notifications from the dietitian
  • ✅ Basic nutritional tips (static)

⭐ Pro Plan — €5.99/month

Exclusive features that competitors don't offer for free, or don't offer at all.

  • ⭐ Unlimited food diary (full history)
  • ⭐ Detailed micronutrients (vitamins, minerals)
  • ⭐ Nutritional trend analysis (weekly/monthly charts)
  • ⭐ PDF reports to send to the dietitian
  • ⭐ Monitoring of condition-specific parameters (blood glucose, creatinine, etc.)
  • ⭐ Personalized reminders (medication, weight, meals)
  • ⭐ Wearable integration (Fitbit, Garmin, Apple Health)
  • ⭐ Adherence analysis for the dietitian's plan (% of weeks)
  • ⭐ Saved personal recipes
  • ⭐ AI food-substitution suggestions
